[DevSheets](/projects/devsheets) now has a nightly validation run. It checks
reference sheets against current documentation, using Context7 as one of
its sources, and writes reports for review.

The boundary matters: the routine researches and reports. It doesn't edit
the content database or silently publish the changes it thinks are needed.
A reported broken or outdated example is a finding to investigate, not an
automatically accepted correction.

The first scheduled run completed in about eleven minutes and produced
reports for DOM manipulation, MongoDB, and Alpine.js. I checked that the
content database's counts and sampled update and validation timestamps
were unchanged, and the code checkout was still clean afterward.

Interactive validation can also reuse a recent nightly report rather than
repeat the research, with an option to force a fresh pass. That shortcut
hasn't had its own real-sheet check yet. The nightly routine always does
fresh research.

I also wrote a plain guide for the routine: what runs, where the reports
land, and what still needs a person. Getting a scheduled job to finish is
one part; being able to understand what it did the next morning is another.
