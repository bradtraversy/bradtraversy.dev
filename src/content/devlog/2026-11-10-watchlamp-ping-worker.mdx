[Watchlamp](/projects/watchlamp) can now record a ping and show it in a check's
history. Accounts and creating the first cron check were already in place;
this adds the path a scheduled job will call to say it ran.

A Cloudflare Worker sits in front of the app. Locally, I tested it forwarding
pings with a shared secret, rejecting forged forwarding headers, limiting
requests, and capping stored messages. The implementation is merged, but
the Worker isn't deployed to its production domain yet.

One small trap: Hono runs GET handlers for HEAD requests too. That's useful
for normal pages, but a HEAD request shouldn't record a successful cron run.
The ping route now checks the method explicitly.

The history page also needed to handle long, unbroken messages without
stretching its table across a phone screen. Those little details matter
when the thing you're displaying is arbitrary job output.

The next slice is the alarm evaluator. Recording that a job called home is
working locally. Deciding that it has gone missing, and eventually notifying
someone, is still ahead. Watchlamp is in development, not launched.
