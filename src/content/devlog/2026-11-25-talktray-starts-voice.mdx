TalkTray has moved beyond the keyword detector proof. On the Mac, a wake
phrase can now bring up an assistant's app and start its voice conversation
through the accessibility controls.

With the assistant apps already running, the spoken checks passed five out
of five for each assistant. I also checked voice already being on,
Accessibility permission being off and restored, and calling Claude from
its Code tab.

Starting a closed app is a different problem. In the final cold-start runs,
ChatGPT and Grok Bot ignored the first press of the voice control. A short
wait could report failure while voice was still coming up. I chose separate
waits: three seconds when the app was already running, eight when TalkTray
had just started it.

Those cold starts were slower, and spoken wake-word cold starts still need
their own check. I don't want to turn the good already-running results into
a claim that every launch is quick and reliable.

The launcher is merged, with the macOS verification passing. It's still a
build in progress, not a released tray app. The useful milestone is that the
wake phrase now reaches the assistant's actual voice interface.
