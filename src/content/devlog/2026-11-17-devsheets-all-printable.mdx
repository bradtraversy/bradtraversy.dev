Every sheet in [DevSheets](/projects/devsheets) now has a printable PDF.
The last two batches added 17, bringing the catalog to 91 out of 91.
That goes beyond the language PDFs I added earlier: the collection now covers
the framework, tooling, API, and narrower reference sheets too.

The work wasn't just exporting browser pages. Each PDF has an editable source,
and I reviewed every page in the final batches as a rendered image. I also
re-rendered the older files to check that finishing the collection hadn't
changed PDFs that were already done.

The production check covered the downloads and their previews, plus the PDF
card on every sheet page. All 91 cards were present, and the last 17 downloads
matched the committed files.

There is still content maintenance to do. A complete set of PDFs doesn't mean
every underlying sheet has had a fresh technical validation pass. Some of the
fast-moving tools and frameworks need that follow-up, and changed sheets will
need their PDFs kept in sync.

The full collection is at [devsheets.io/pdfs](https://devsheets.io/pdfs).
