Rehearsium is a conversation rehearsal app I'm building. The job interview
scenario now covers more than developer roles: tech, business and finance,
healthcare, sales and service, and teaching.

The start screen opens with empty custom fields rather than an example that
looks like something you've already entered. Pick a role type to fill in a
starting point, then choose Mixed, Behavioral, or Role skills as the focus.
Your edits survive switching between the cards and Custom, and the interview
remembers its choices separately from the podcast scenario.

Underneath, the role cards and podcast show types now use the same template
structure. Each scenario says which text fields a template fills, and the
validation catches templates that point to fields that don't exist.

The change is merged. The 332-test verification passed, and I checked the
start flow, saved choices, keyboard selection, and narrow layout in the
browser. The real-microphone check for a business interview focused on role
skills is still outstanding. I haven't verified that the interviewer stays
away from software questions in that live conversation yet.
