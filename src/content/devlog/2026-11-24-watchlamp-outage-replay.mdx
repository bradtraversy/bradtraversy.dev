[Watchlamp](/projects/watchlamp) can now keep incoming pings while its main
application is unavailable. The ping Worker saves them in Cloudflare D1, and
the app collects them before its next evaluation run.

The important part is judging a replayed ping at its original time. A job
that ran on schedule during the app's outage shouldn't produce a false alarm
when the app returns. A job that really ran late still needs its Down and Up
events, even if both are discovered afterward.

There's also an Unknown state. If Watchlamp can't safely decide whether a
check is late or down, it says so rather than treating a problem with the
monitor as proof that the job failed. Evaluation stays a little behind the
Worker's clock so an in-flight ping isn't prematurely counted as missing.

I watched the outage demo locally with the Worker in front of the app. The
feature passed 498 unit tests and 46 browser tests, then deployed with the
new Worker and database. Production checks confirmed the app was collecting
saved pings, but I haven't deliberately caused a real production outage.
