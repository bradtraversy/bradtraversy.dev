[AI Blueprint](/projects/ai-blueprint) 1.16.1 is released. This patch trims the
instructions loaded during an ordinary feature loop without changing what
the workflow asks the agent to do.

Two references were doing more work than their names suggested. Normal
completion loaded the full recovery guide just to capture the source tree,
and implementation loaded the whole Try It Now guide. The smaller references
now cover what those steps actually need.

The other half of the cleanup was deciding what to leave alone. I kept
Autopilot and Continuous because they stop at different places. Autopilot
builds one feature and stops for review before completion. Continuous works
through features and merges each checked result locally. Neither mode's job
is to push the changes for you.

I also left the separate Continuous quality settings and the remaining
activity tracking in place. Removing a few configuration lines wasn't worth
spreading another change through the parser, dashboard, commands, and tests.

The release passed the package checks, 223 tests, and CI on Node 22, Node 24,
and Windows. This closes the slim-down pass. Fewer instructions are useful;
fewer capabilities aren't automatically an improvement.
