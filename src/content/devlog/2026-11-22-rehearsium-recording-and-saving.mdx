Rehearsium now saves the audio from a conversation rehearsal alongside a
timeline of the partner's turns. That gives the later analysis a recording
and a record of when the other person was speaking.

I kept the live transcript limited to the partner's lines. My own words will
come from a separate analysis pass, rather than adding another transcription
model to the live conversation.

The saving flow has to survive less tidy endings too: a dropped connection,
the time limit, an upload failure, or leaving before the save finishes.
An empty recording gets its own message. Reloading before an unsaved recording
has been stored still loses that in-memory recording, so the page warns before
navigation instead of pretending the audio is already safe.

I checked a real conversation in Patient mode. The decoded recording length
matched the saved record within a tenth of a second, and the partner's turns
fell inside the silences in my recording. Upload checks also confirmed that
a file over the size limit is refused without leaving a partial file.

That hands-on check covered Patient mode. Natural, Manual, changing modes
midway, and a system-muted microphone still needed their own checks at this
point. Recording and saving are merged; the finished product isn't released.
